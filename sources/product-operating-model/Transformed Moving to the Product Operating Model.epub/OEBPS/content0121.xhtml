PART VIII

Transformation Techniques


Now that we've discussed what it means to transform to the product operating model, the new product model competencies, and the new product model concepts, and we've shared several examples of companies that have successfully transformed, we are ready to discuss the more general topic of change management.


In other words, what is the best way of getting from where you are now to where you want to go?


Depending on the size of the organization working to transform, it can take anywhere from six months to two years to move to the product operating model, and that's assuming the company is serious about the transformation and it's not just theater.


The seven chapters in this part are all about sharing the techniques we've found helpful for working through your transformation.
