CHAPTER 11

Product Designers


Most companies transforming to the product model already have some number of designers, but they typically play a very different role than what's needed in the product model.


In the prior models, designers too often play a service role to a product manager, or they support the marketing organization, none of which is what we need.


In terms of what we need in the product model, there are really two issues here: how the product works, and product discovery.
