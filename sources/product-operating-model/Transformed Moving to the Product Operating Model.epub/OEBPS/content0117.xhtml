The Problem to Solve


Fortunately, the company's product organization had been learning and testing many fitness-related product ideas with their users, and they had already discovered that a significant portion of users were interested in wellness activities beyond gym-based fitness, such as meditation, mindfulness, nutrition, and home-based exercise.


The problem the product teams needed to solve was to provide a range of home-based wellness solutions to Gympass users so they could maintain their fitness, even without access to a gym or studio.


Strategically, the product team took a build-and-partner approach—building key parts to the solution but partnering with other fitness-solution providers for specific vertical fitness solutions.
