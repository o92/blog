Key Product Decisions


Product decisions come up all the time in product discovery, and for many of those you will want to consult with your PMM partner. But for especially important decisions, because the PMM has such deep knowledge of the target markets and the product's go-to-market, the product team will want to be sure to include the PMM's perspective.


To be clear, a product's go-to-market is one of the most important considerations in determining successful product strategies.
