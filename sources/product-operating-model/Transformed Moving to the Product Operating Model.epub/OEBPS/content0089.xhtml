Product Discovery


The purpose of product discovery is to come up with an effective solution to your customers' problems. Your product team strives to discover solutions that your customers love, yet work for your business.


To do this, the product team has a cross-functional set of skills in product management, product design, and engineering. The product team engages directly with users and customers to deeply understand the problem that needs to be solved, and to test out potential solutions.


For the majority of direct customer interactions, the product team focuses both on understanding the customer's problem and on determining if you have come up with a valuable, usable, feasible, and viable solution.


As such, most of the direct interactions between the product team and the customer involve interviewing the different users and testing prototypes of solutions with those users.
