## Catalysts（3.4）

1. **What do you mean by X?**  
   会议里对业务专名追问含义。很多混乱沟通，靠对齐领域概念就能化解。

2. **假设 / 问题 / 冲突写便签**  
   协作建模时把假设、疑问、冲突贴出来，显式可见。

3. **从中抽出并澄清 heuristics**  
   从人们的假设、问题与「好设计」建议里，提炼并澄清其启发式。把启发式说出口，本身就是过程的一部分。

## 可抄进 journal 的章末启发式（3.5，摘要）

**Design**

- Split according to the language / departments / actors  
- Communication with external systems happens in a separate bounded context  
- Optimize for future potential  
- Split bounded contexts based on how it would happen in a paper world  
- What changes together, gets deployed together / stays together  
- If we’re still deploying the bounded contexts together after two years, we merge them into one  

**Guiding**

- Do sensemaking to test your assumptions  
- Ask who can relate to (even part of) what was just discussed  
- Indicate emerging Pivotal Events；Create different groups between Pivotal Events  
- Add minority wisdom to the group  
- Discuss conflicts with the entire group；Split and merge during diverging conversations  

（可改、可补；当自己 journal 的种子即可。）
